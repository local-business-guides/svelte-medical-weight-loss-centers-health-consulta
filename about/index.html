About Svelte Medical Weight Loss Centers, Orlando.

Svelte Medical Weight Loss CentersUpdated 2026-11-20

Svelte Medical Weight Loss Centers has helped patients across Central Florida since 2008 with physician-supervised programs tailored to individual needs. Our providers and nutrition specialists focus on fat loss, improved energy, and long-term wellness. Services include GLP-1 medications such as Semaglutide and Tirzepatide, prescription options such as Phentermine, personalized Bio-identical Hormone Optimization through the Biote pellet method, and B-12 shots.

We aim to make it straightforward to learn about your options. You can contact us with questions about our services, share the health goals you’d like to address, and speak with our team about available care. A provider can help explain which options may be appropriate for your needs.

Visit us at 7009 Dr Phillips Blvd #240, Orlando, FL 32819, or call +1407-804-5200. Our hours are Monday through Wednesday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM; Thursday, 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM; and Friday, 7:30 AM to noon. We’re closed Saturday and Sunday.
